Glass, shape, motion

One recipe on macOS, Windows, Linux and mobile. Wubble draws its own glass instead of asking the OS for it, so it looks the same everywhere.

Thin Tooltips, chips, URL pill blur 16 · sat 160%
fill white 8%
shadow 10 / 24
Regular Sidebar, toolbar, dock blur 30 · sat 180%
fill #161921 42% + Space tint
shadow 18 / 40
Thick Menus, popovers, command bar blur 44 · sat 200%
fill #1C1F28 64%
shadow 24 / 60 · film rim on

Glass, layer by layer

  1. Backdrop: blur, then saturate whatever sits behind.
  2. Tint: the tier's fill, plus the Space colour at 10% on sidebar glass.
  3. Specular: white 10% to 0 across the top 42%.
  4. Rim: 1 px inner highlight (white 22% top, 7% all round).
  5. Film rim: 1.5 px soap-film ring. Only on hover, press, drag and focus, so the sheen reads as touch.
  6. Shadow: soft drop, grows with tier.

Light theme keeps the layers and flips the fills: white 55 / 68 / 80%, ink #11131A, Sky deepens to #1E6BD6 for 4.7:1 text.
